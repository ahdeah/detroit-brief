Privacy policy

Last updated 26 October 2026.

"Detroit brief" is a small application run by one person for his own use. It uploads spoken briefs to his own YouTube channel through Google's YouTube Data API, using a sign-in he grants to his own Google account.

  • It is used by its owner only. It has no users, no sign-up and no accounts of its own.
  • It collects, stores and shares no personal information about anyone. The only Google data it touches is the owner's own channel: it uploads videos to it and sets their titles, descriptions and visibility.
  • The sign-in token Google issues stays on the owner's own computer and is used only to perform those uploads. It is never sent anywhere else.
  • The briefs themselves are built from public statistics and public documents and contain no personal data about private individuals.
  • Its use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Questions: the support address shown on the application's Google sign-in screen.
