Detroit brief

A monthly spoken brief on Detroit, its six-county metro and the geographies that bear on it: the economy, the city's finances, education, the determinants of health, and community. Every number in it is taken from a public source by software and pinned to the period it covers and the date it was released; the written brief behind each cut carries every source, date and quote.

The cuts are published on this YouTube channel. They are read by a synthetic voice, and no study has yet tested whether a briefing heard this way is as accurate or as trusted as one read.

This site is the home page of the small application that uploads the cuts to that channel. It collects nothing from anyone. See the privacy policy and terms.
